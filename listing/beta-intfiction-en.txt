Beta readers wanted: Wayward, an illustrated gamebook for Android (49 pages, 19 endings)

Hi all. I'm Manuel, and I've been building Wayward: a gamebook for phones that tries to feel like reading an old leather-bound book rather than tapping through menus. You turn pages by dragging the corner, the text appears at reading pace, and a few scenes let you reach into the world around the book (wiping a misted window, pulling a bell rope).

Book I, The Hollow Mountain, has 49 pages and 19 endings, two of them true endings. It's a classic branching structure, closer to Fighting Fantasy and Choose Your Own Adventure than to parser IF, in English and Dutch.

It's in an open beta on Google Play: [BETALINK]
Android only for now: tap "Become a tester", then install it from the Play Store.

What I'd love to hear, as bluntly as you like:
- Do the choices feel meaningful, or are some of them fake?
- Where does the pace drag?
- Endings that feel unearned or abrupt
- Anything that breaks, typos, awkward English

There's a "Give feedback" button in Settings and on every ending screen, or just reply here. I'll read everything and post what I changed.

Thanks!
