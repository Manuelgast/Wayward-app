Your mentor vanished inside a mountain. Her last words to you: don’t follow me.

Wayward is a library of illustrated adventure books in which you decide what happens next. Every page ends with a choice, and every choice turns you to a different page. Some roads lead to glory. Others lead to the bottom of a well.

FOUR BOOKS ON THE SHELF
Book I is completely free. Every other book starts with free endings.

BOOK I · THE HOLLOW MOUNTAIN
Follow the royal cartographer Edda Marlow to Mount Veyra, a mountain that appears on no map. Wander through a village where every lantern has the same dent, a hall with a lantern for every name, and a black river that returns everything it takes.
• 49 pages, 19 endings, two of them true endings, and 12 hidden relics

BOOK II · THE DROWNED LIGHTHOUSE
Every night at two, a lighthouse that sank a hundred years ago switches on beneath the sea. Edda’s trail leads you to Grey Harbour, a ledger that never balances, and a ship that is still trying to come home.
• 49 pages, 19 endings, two of them true endings, and 14 hidden relics

A BOOK THAT COMES ALIVE
• An old leather-bound book with pages you turn yourself, set inside moving, illustrated scenes
• Mist, sea spray, ash or stardust that rolls off the page and drifts away around your fingers
• Interactive moments: wipe a misted window, raise your lantern in the dark, draw a bridge across the void, pull the bell rope
• Now and then the world stirs: water drips onto the page, eyes open in the dark
• Timed choices: hesitate too long and the fog chooses for you
• Music and recorded sound for every place you visit, and text that appears at reading pace

SECRETS IN THE MARGINS
• Hidden relics in every book, tucked into the text and the scenery, each with its own piece of lore
• Secret choices that only appear once you know enough
• A map of every path you have taken, and the ones still in the fog
• Keep a finger between the pages and go back to any choice

READ YOUR WAY

• Adjustable text speed, music and sound effects
